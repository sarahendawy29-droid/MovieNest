Overview

Squid Game follows a group of people who are struggling financially and are invited to
participate in a mysterious competition.They are promised a huge cash prize, but they soon discover that the seemingly simple
children's games have deadly consequences.
The series also explores money, inequality,
desperation, human nature, and how far people are willing to go for survival.
