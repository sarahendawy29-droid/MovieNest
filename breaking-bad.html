Overview

Breaking Bad follows a high-school chemistry teacher whose life changes after he is diagnosed with cancer.
Worried about his family's financial future, he starts producing illegal drugs
with a former student.As his criminal business grows,
he becomes increasingly powerful and dangerous, while his personal life begins to fall apart.
