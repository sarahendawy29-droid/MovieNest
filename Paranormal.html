Overview

Paranormal follows Dr. Refaat Ismail, a skeptical hematologist whose life takes an unexpected turn when he
begins experiencing a series of strange and supernatural events. Set in 1960s Egypt, Refaat investigates
mysterious cases involving ghosts, curses, and unexplained phenomena while questioning everything he
believes about the world. The series combines Egyptian folklore, horror, mystery, and suspense as Refaat
faces increasingly terrifying encounters.
