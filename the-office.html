The Office

2005

|9 Season | 201 Episodes | Comedy | 8.5

Main Cast

Steve Carell | Rainn Wilson | John Krasinski | Jenna Fischer | B.J. Novak

Overview

The Office is a comedy about the everyday lives of employees working at a paper company office in Pennsylvania.
The show follows their friendships, relationships, workplace drama, awkward moments,
and ridiculous situations. It's filmed in a mockumentary style,
as if a documentary crew is filming their real lives.
