Overview

Succession follows the powerful Roy family, owners of a global media and entertainment empire, as they struggle
over who will take control of the company when their aging father begins to consider his future. The series explores
family rivalry, corporate power, ambition, betrayal, and the complicated relationships between the Roy siblings. As
each member fights for influence and their father's approval, alliances constantly shift and the battle for control
becomes increasingly ruthless.
