Overview

Balto follows a young doctor who has just graduated from medical uni
and is unexpectedly assigned to work at a rural health unit in a small Egyptian village.
When he arrives, he discovers that the place is completely different from what he expected.
He gets caught up in strange situations, eccentric people, and the challenges of working as a doctor in a remote community.
