Overview

Friends follows the lives of six close friends living in New York City as they navigate relationships, careers,
friendship, and everyday life together. Through breakups, new jobs, family problems, and countless
situations, their friendship remains at the center of their lives. The series combines comedy, romance,
and memorable moments as the friends grow and experience life together.
