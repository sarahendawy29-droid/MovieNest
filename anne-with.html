Overview

Anne with an E is a Canadian drama series based on the novel Anne of Green Gables by Lucy Maud Montgomery.
The series follows Anne Shirley, an imaginative and intelligent orphan
who is adopted by Marilla and Matthew Cuthbert and goes to live with them in Avonlea.
