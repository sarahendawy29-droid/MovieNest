Overview

Rafat Al-Haggan tells the story of a young Egyptian man who is recruited by Egyptian intelligence
and sent to Israel on a dangerous undercover mission. He builds a new identity and gains the trust of
important figures while secretly gathering valuable information for Egypt. The series follows his
difficult journey as he faces danger, betrayal, and the constant risk of being discovered.
