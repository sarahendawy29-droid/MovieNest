Overview

Share' Al Asha is set in a neighborhood in 1970s Saudi Arabia and follows several families whose lives become interconnected through
love, marriage, family conflicts, social expectations, and secrets.
The story especially explores the lives of women and the restrictions they face within a
traditional society, while also following the relationships and conflicts between different families in the neighborhood.
